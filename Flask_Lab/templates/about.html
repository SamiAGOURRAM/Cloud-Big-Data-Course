ℹ️ About This Project

📖 Overview

This is a machine learning web application that predicts the species of Iris flowers based on their physical measurements. The application uses the famous Iris dataset, which contains measurements of sepal and petal dimensions for three different species of Iris flowers.

🌸 Iris Species

  • Versicolor: Medium-sized petals and sepals
  • Virginica: Largest petals and longer sepals
